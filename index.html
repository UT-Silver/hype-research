01结论Key findings

  1. 哑铃型结构，中间薄。底部是不会卖或已经销毁的零/低成本筹码：团队地址 238.15M 枚（成本为零，全部质押）、Assistance Fund 48.81M 枚（回购均价 $29.32，已认定销毁），另有 410.03M 枚 未来排放尚未分配。流通盘里非团队质押 200.57M 枚，真正在场内换手的活跃流通盘只有约 101.20M 枚。顶部则是 9 月以来在高位换手的新筹码。A barbell with a thin middle. At the bottom sits zero- or low-cost supply that will not sell or is already burned: the team address holds 238.15M HYPE (zero cost, fully staked), the Assistance Fund 48.81M HYPE (buyback average $29.32, recognized as burned), and another 410.03M HYPE of future emissions is unallocated. Of circulating supply, 200.57M HYPE is staked by non-team holders, leaving an active float of only about 101.20M HYPE. The top is new supply that changed hands at high prices since September.
  2. 活跃筹码重心已经上移到高位，现价跌到了均价下方。活跃筹码均价 $71.84，70% 的筹码落在 $55.75 到 $87.25，最大单峰在 $81.25。获利比例 10 月 31 日还是 85.0%，现在 42.0%，是 9 月以来最低：过去十天的下跌正好穿过了最密的筹码区。7–8 月平台整理时曾低至 27.1%；历史上跌到接近零（2025 年 4 月、12 月和 2026 年 1 月）才对应阶段性底部。The active cost center has moved up, and price has slipped below it. Active-supply average cost is $71.84; 70% of it sits between $55.75 and $87.25, and the largest cluster is at $81.25. The share in profit fell from 85.0% on Oct 31 to 42.0% now, the lowest since September: the ten-day sell-off cut straight through the densest cost zone. It bottomed at 27.1% during the July–August range; historically only readings near zero (Apr 2025, Dec 2025, Jan 2026) marked cycle lows.
  3. 下方第一道“有人捍卫”的成本带。Hyperliquid Strategies 披露截至 10 月 28 日持有约 38.90M 枚，综合成本 $56.35；但它 7–9 月新买的约 9.10M 枚 成本约 $86.26，这部分已浮亏约 16.9%。ETF 建仓窗口均价约 $61.46（持仓约 4.68M 枚，按 8 月初规模估），链上现货持有人成本中位数 $57.68。三者叠在一起。The first cost band someone will defend. Hyperliquid Strategies disclosed about 38.90M HYPE as of Oct 28 at a $56.35 blended cost, but the roughly 9.10M HYPE it bought in July–September cost about $86.26 and is now about 16.9% under water. The ETF entry window averages about $61.46 (holdings about 4.68M HYPE, estimated from early-August AUM), and the median on-chain spot holder cost is $57.68. The three overlap.
  4. 团队解锁的抛压在链上没有兑现。按 24 个月线性理论上每月 9.92M 枚，团队地址一年累计实际只分出 9.72M 枚，还少于它同期拿到的质押奖励 9.87M 枚，所以地址余额基本没动。10 月单月分出 3.75M 枚，收款方汇总后把其中 3.13M 枚 重新质押，其余转去 HyperEVM 侧，HyperCore 上没有现货卖出记录；11 月 6 日只分出 0.18M 枚。风险在于这批零成本筹码的释放节奏由团队自由裁量。Team unlock selling has not shown up on-chain. A 24-month linear schedule implies 9.92M HYPE a month, yet the team address has distributed only 9.72M HYPE in total over a year, less than the 9.87M HYPE of staking rewards it earned, so its balance has barely moved. Of October's 3.75M HYPE, recipients consolidated and re-staked 3.13M HYPE and sent the rest to HyperEVM, with no spot sales on HyperCore; Nov 6 saw only 0.18M HYPE. The risk is that the release pace of this zero-cost supply is at the team's discretion.
  5. 衍生品端空头在加仓。HYPE 永续未平仓 21.34M 枚，相当于活跃流通盘的 21.1%；资金费率转负，快照年化约 -20.7%，空头在付费。快照值波动大，但方向说明下跌里有不少是新开空单。Shorts are adding in the perp market. HYPE perp open interest is 21.34M HYPE, 21.1% of the active float, and funding has flipped negative, about -20.7% annualized at the snapshot, so shorts are paying. Snapshot funding is noisy, but the sign says a good part of the drop came from new shorts.
  6. 买盘与长期中枢。AF 两次快照之间（约 36 小时）回购约 $3.06M，折合每天约 $2.04M，买入均价 $73.90。上线以来 VWAP $30.26 和 AF 回购均价 $29.32 几乎重合；365 日 VWAP $45.80，HyperCore 现货“已实现价格” $51.58。The bid and the long-run anchors. Between two snapshots about 36 hours apart the AF bought roughly $3.06M, about $2.04M a day, at an average $73.90. The all-time VWAP of $30.26 almost matches the AF buyback average of $29.32; the 365D VWAP is $45.80 and the HyperCore spot “realized price” is $51.58.

05方法与口径Method and definitions

价格与成交量
Hyperliquid 现货 HYPE/USDC（@107）日线，2024-11-29 至今，成交量以 HYPE 计；最后一根为当日未收盘数据。未纳入 CEX 现货和永续成交，因此换手率偏低估、做市商对倒偏高估。
筹码分布（换手衰减）
每天的成交量按当日最高—最低价之间的三角分布（峰值在 (高+低+收)/3）落到各价位，旧筹码按当日换手率 = 成交量 ÷ 流通盘 同比例衰减；创世筹码按零成本起算。活跃口径的流通盘 = 流通量 − 非团队质押；全流通口径用全部流通量。模型假设所有持有人换手概率相同，会低估从不交易的空投老户和质押者，所以只代表“在场内流动的筹码”。
VWAP 成本线
典型价 (高+低+收)/3 按成交量加权，窗口分别为上线以来、365 日、180 日、90 日。
链上成本（entryNtl）
Hyperliquid 为每个地址的现货余额记录 entryNtl；买入按成交价、转入按到账时的价格计，类似比特币的“已实现价格”。只覆盖 HyperCore 现货余额，质押和 HyperEVM 侧的币没有可比的成本记录。
机构成本
Hyperliquid Strategies 的持仓量与综合成本取自公司季度披露（截至 10 月 28 日）；7–9 月新增部分成本 = 披露的买入金额 ÷ 买入数量。ETF 成本为 5 月 12 日至 7 月 16 日（净流入集中期）的 HL VWAP 代理，持仓量按 8 月初三只 ETF 的规模除以当时币价估算，之后的流入流出未计。
没覆盖的
CEX 托管余额与成本、HyperEVM 侧（DeFi、LST 抵押）的成本、非团队质押者的个体成本、Hyperion DeFi 等小型 DAT 的成本，以及解押队列的实时规模。资金费率、AF 回购速度为快照值。
Price and volume
Hyperliquid spot HYPE/USDC (@107) daily candles from 2024-11-29, volume in HYPE; the last candle is today and still open. CEX spot and perp volume are excluded, so turnover is understated while market-maker wash flow is overstated.
Distribution (turnover decay)
Each day’s volume is spread across that day’s low–high range with a triangular distribution peaking at (H+L+C)/3; existing chips decay in proportion to turnover = volume ÷ float; genesis supply starts at zero cost. Active float = circulating − non-team staked; full float uses all circulating supply. The model assumes every holder is equally likely to trade, so it understates airdrop holders and stakers who never trade. It describes the supply that actually moves.
VWAP cost lines
Typical price (H+L+C)/3 weighted by volume, over all-time, 365, 180 and 90 days.
On-chain cost (entryNtl)
Hyperliquid records entryNtl for each address’s spot balance: buys at the fill price, transfers in at the arrival price, much like Bitcoin’s realized price. It covers HyperCore spot balances only; staked and HyperEVM tokens have no comparable cost record.
Institutional cost
Hyperliquid Strategies’ holdings and blended cost come from its quarterly release (as of Oct 28); the July–September tranche cost = disclosed spend ÷ tokens bought. ETF cost is proxied by the HL VWAP from May 12 to Jul 16, when net inflows concentrated; holdings are estimated from the three ETFs’ early-August AUM divided by the price then, and later flows are not included.
Not covered
CEX custody balances and cost, HyperEVM-side cost (DeFi, LST collateral), individual staker cost, smaller DATs such as Hyperion DeFi, and the live unstaking queue. Funding and the AF buyback pace are snapshot values.
